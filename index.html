The Solution: BioStride

Revolutionary Biosensor Platform for Point-of-Care Diagnostics

BioStride democratizes early disease detection with a universal, handheld platform using modular transistor-based test strips. Detect multiple diseases—oral cancer, HIV, hepatitis, and more—from a single drop of saliva or blood.
  • Portable, affordable, and lab-free—runs anywhere, anytime
  • Detects well-studied biomarkers (e.g., CIP2A for oral cancer, p24 for HIV, HBsAg for hepatitis B) using click chemistry
  • Instant results via smartphone app—track, share, and act in minutes
  • Costs less than ₹100 per test—accessible for all
